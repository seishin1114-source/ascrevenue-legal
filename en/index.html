Revenue for App Store Connect

By Miyajin Product

See your App Store downloads and proceeds, and compare them with the period before.

Using your own App Store Connect API key, the app fetches Apple's sales reports straight to your device. There is no developer server. Your key is stored only in your device's Keychain, and your sales data only on your device.

Dashboard

Downloads and proceeds for the latest day, the last 7 days, this week, last week, this month, last month or a custom range, compared with the period before.

Analysis

Breakdowns and trends by country, by app and by type (first-time downloads, redownloads, in-app purchases and more).

My Page

Totals for the year you imported, your best month, and the apps and countries that grew the most.

Demo

Try every screen with made-up data, no API key needed.
